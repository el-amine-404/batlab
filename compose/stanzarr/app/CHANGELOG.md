# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Library tracking: songs, every lyrics file and format, status, language, origin.
- LRCLIB: lookup of new songs, monthly retries, synced lyrics preferred.
- AI sync through a GPU worker: Demucs, Whisper (two sizes) and MMS, median vote, unsure lines marked.
- Editor: song and voice waveforms, snap to the voice, nudges, karaoke preview, drag, duplicate, undo.
- Dashboard, library, queue, workers and settings pages; one account; read-only mode.
